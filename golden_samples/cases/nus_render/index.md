### Doctor of Engineering (Built Environment)
Designed for students aiming to advance their expertise in project, facilities, contract, and technology management within the built environment sector, this doctorate programme emphasises industry-driven research, with students typically working on projects initiated by their employers. The programme integrates substantial coursework in technology management, preparing graduates for leadership roles in technology innovation, enterprise, and integrated planning and management. Research opportunities span areas such as project management, digital construction, sustainable buildings, project finance, and building energy efficiency.

### Doctor of Medicine (MD)
This is a joint degree programme with a medical curriculum modeled on Duke University's approach but tailored to the Singapore context. It emphasises team-based learning and integrates extensive research training alongside clinical education. The programme comprises four phases: pre-clerkship, clerkship, research, and advanced clinical rotations. It aims to produce physician scientists who are not only skilled clinicians but also innovative contributors to medical research and leadership.
Intake Period:Jan; AugMode of Study:Full-Time; Part-Time
