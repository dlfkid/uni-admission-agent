Admissions to Master's Programmes in 2026/27 begin on 16 September 2025.
Applicants whose entrance qualification is obtained from an institution where the medium of instruction is NOT English should also fulfill the minimum English proficiency requirement. They are required to provide their English test results obtained within the two years preceding the commencement of the University's application period, i.e. TOEFL and IELTS Tests taken on or after 16 September 2023 is acceptable for 2026 admissions, except for P45 PCLL programme. Please refer to [the English Proficiency Requirements](https://www.cityu.edu.hk/pg/taught-postgraduate-programmes/entrance-requirements) for details.
