Admission Requirements

In addition to the general requirements of the Graduate School, applicants should submit a statement of purpose (one page).

Application Deadline

1st round: 30 September 2025
2nd round: 31 October 2025
3rd round: 30 November 2025
4th round: 31 December 2025
5th round: 31 January 2026

Applications will be processed on a rolling basis from October 2025 until all places have been filled. Therefore, early applications are strongly encouraged. Applications submitted after 31 December 2025 may be considered, subject to availability of places.
